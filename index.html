Privacy Policy

What we keep: the email of your Google account (so each person has one account), your country (worked out from your connection), and what you choose under "I am".

Video and sound go directly between you and the person you meet. We do not record or store them.

What the other person sees: your video and your country. They cannot see your email or your "I am" choice.

Reports: if someone reports you, we keep a record of it to help keep everyone safe, and repeated reports can lead to a ban.

Your data: we do not sell it. To ask about or delete your data, contact the site owner.
